# Community conduct

EcoLogic contributors and collaborators are expected to make participation
safe, respectful, and technically honest.

Be constructive, specific, and welcoming. Respect privacy, consent,
accessibility needs, cultural and language differences, and the work and rights
of others. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Do not harass, threaten,
impersonate, discriminate, publish private information, or pressure anyone to
disclose confidential material.

Security research must stay within authorized systems and use private
reporting. Product claims, test results, attribution, and authorship must be
accurate. Generated content does not excuse plagiarism or unsafe conduct.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict access, or
escalate serious conduct that conflicts with these expectations. Report conduct
concerns privately to the repository owner through an already established
channel; do not expose sensitive details in an issue.
