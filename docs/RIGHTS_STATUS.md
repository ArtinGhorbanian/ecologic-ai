# Rights and publication status

- Status: **private-only / not cleared for public proprietary publication**
- Reviewed: 2026-08-08

## Evidence found

- The original source contained one personal developer attribution. It is
  preserved only in an ignored local provenance record and is not repeated in
  public-facing files.
- No Git history, prior license, contributor agreement, assignment, team list,
  employer/client notice, sponsor terms, NDA, embargo, starter-repository
  notice, dataset terms, or competition record was present in the workspace.
- No competition or hackathon origin was detected. Because no event was
  identified, no organizer terms could be reviewed and no
  `COMPETITION_DISCLOSURE.md` was created.
- The legacy raster assistant image has no verified source/license and is
  excluded. Its replacement is an independently authored abstract SVG.
- The bundled Plus Jakarta Sans files identify the SIL Open Font License 1.1;
  the required copyright and license text are included.

Absence of evidence is not chain-of-title clearance. A public release or an
exclusive AIONEX copyright claim would be unsupported today.

## Required written attestation before public release

The verified rights holder must confirm, with supporting records where
applicable:

- who created every material portion of the original project;
- whether any teammate, employer, client, school, sponsor, or contractor has
  ownership or approval rights;
- whether the project entered any competition, hackathon, challenge, course,
  grant, or sponsored program and, if so, the complete governing terms;
- whether any NDA, embargo, judging restriction, assignment, or prize condition
  applies;
- that all starter code, datasets, prompts, icons, copy, and assets are either
  original or redistributable under documented terms;
- that public and commercial publication is authorized; and
- whether AIONEX may accurately be named as the copyright holder, or whether
  shared/contributor wording is required.

## Release rule

Until the checklist is complete, keep the repository private, use the interim
root `LICENSE`, avoid organizer/trademark/award claims, and do not publish the
prepared marketing copy. After clearance, have qualified counsel or the
verified owner approve the final proprietary notice and all required
attributions before changing visibility.
